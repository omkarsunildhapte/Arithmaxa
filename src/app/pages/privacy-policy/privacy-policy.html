Arithmaxa is built with a privacy-first approach. Your calculations stay on your device. We don't run ads, and we don't sell your data.

Local First History & settings stay on your device only
AI Chat Answered on-device first — cloud only as fallback

1. What We Collect

Stored Only On Your Device

All of the following is kept in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to us:

  • Calculation History — Your recent calculations (up to 20 entries), including expressions, results, and timestamps.
  • App Preferences — Calculator mode (basic / scientific), angle unit (radians / degrees), and display state.
  • Privacy Consent — Your choices from this consent dialog, stored as arithmaxa_consent_v1 in localStorage, and mirrored into a matching device cookie on Android/iOS builds (see Section 8 below).

Sent to Third-Party Services

  • AI Chat Messages — On devices that support it (recent Pixel and iPhone models), your question is answered entirely on-device using Google's or Apple's built-in AI — nothing leaves your phone. On other devices, or whenever on-device processing isn't available, your question and conversation context are instead sent to our own backend server, which forwards them to OpenRouter's API for processing and returns the answer; our backend does not store the message. You can disable AI Chat processing in the cookie preferences.
  • Photos You Attach — If you attach a photo in AI Chat, on-device text recognition reads any text in it first — that never leaves your device. Only if no text can be read from the photo (e.g. a diagram) is the image itself sent through the same path as AI Chat Messages above.
  • Currency Exchange Rates — The currency converter tool may fetch live exchange rates from external financial data APIs. No personal data is included in these requests.
  • Feedback — Feedback you submit (your star rating, category, and message) is sent by email to the Arithmaxa team so we can act on it. No name or email address is attached to it.
  • Analytics (Optional) — Only if you turn on the "Analytics" toggle in your privacy preferences, we use Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to collect anonymous, aggregate usage data — such as which features are used and how often — to help us improve the app. This is off by default and never includes your calculation history, AI Chat messages, or anything you type. You can turn it on or off at any time in the cookie preferences.

2. Device Permissions

  • Microphone — Requested only when you tap the voice input button in AI Chat. Speech is converted to text entirely on your device by the operating system's built-in speech recognition. We do not receive, store, or process audio.
  • Camera — Requested only when you tap the attach-photo button in AI Chat, to take a picture of a problem. See "Photos You Attach" in Section 1 for how that image is handled.
  • Local Storage — Used to persist your history, settings, and consent choices between sessions.

No other device permissions are requested or used.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Deliver calculator and AI assistant functionality.
  • Remember your preferences across sessions.
  • Improve the app using the feedback you send us.

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any way.

4. Third-Party Services

OpenRouter AI AI Chat Feature

When on-device AI isn't available or a photo needs to be read, AI chat messages are sent to our own backend server, which forwards them to OpenRouter and its partner model providers for processing. Please review the OpenRouter Privacy Policy to understand how they handle prompt data. On-device AI (Google's or Apple's built-in models) never involves OpenRouter or our servers at all.

Firebase Analytics Optional — Off by Default

If you enable Analytics in your privacy preferences, anonymous usage data (such as which screens and features you use) is collected via Firebase Analytics, a Google service. This is entirely optional, off by default, and never includes your calculations, AI Chat content, or anything you type. Please review the Google Privacy Policy to understand how Google handles this data.

6. Children's Privacy

Arithmaxa does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided data through AI Chat, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Security

All local data is protected by your device's operating system and browser security. Transmissions to the OpenRouter API use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

8. Local Storage vs Cookies

Arithmaxa primarily uses browser/app localStorage, not traditional HTTP cookies, for your history, settings, and consent choices — this data stays entirely on your device and isn't automatically sent to any server. On Android and iOS builds, your consent decision is also mirrored into a real device cookie (holding the same value as localStorage) so it's available through native cookie storage as well; this cookie is never sent to us or to any server, it stays on your device the same way localStorage does. You can clear both at any time via the "Delete All My Data" button above.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and notify you within the app if the changes are material. Continued use of Arithmaxa after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
