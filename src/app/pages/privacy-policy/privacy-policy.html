Privacy Policy

Arithmaxa is built with a privacy-first approach. Your calculations stay on your device. We don't run ads, and we don't sell your data.

💾 Local First History & settings stay on your device only
🤖 AI Chat Queries sent to OpenRouter — no other servers
🚫 No Ads No analytics, trackers, or ad networks

1. What We Collect

Stored Only On Your Device

All of the following is kept in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to us:

  • Calculation History — Your recent calculations (up to 20 entries), including expressions, results, and timestamps.
  • App Preferences — Calculator mode (basic / scientific), angle unit (radians / degrees), and display state.
  • Privacy Consent — Your choices from this consent dialog, stored as arithmaxa_consent_v1.
  • Feedback — Any feedback you submit is stored locally as arithmaxa_feedback_v1 and never transmitted.

Sent to Third-Party Services

  • AI Chat Messages — When you use the AI Assistant, your math question and the conversation context are sent to OpenRouter's API for AI model processing. OpenRouter handles this data per their own privacy policy. You can disable AI Chat processing in the cookie preferences.
  • Currency Exchange Rates — The currency converter tool may fetch live exchange rates from external financial data APIs. No personal data is included in these requests.

2. Device Permissions

  • Microphone — Requested only when you tap the voice input button in AI Chat. Audio is processed entirely by your browser's built-in Web Speech API. We do not receive, store, or process audio.
  • Local Storage — Used to persist your history, settings, and consent choices between sessions.

No other device permissions are requested or used.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Deliver calculator and AI assistant functionality.
  • Remember your preferences across sessions.
  • Improve the app using locally stored feedback (never transmitted).

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any way.

4. Third-Party Services

OpenRouter AI AI Chat Feature

AI chat messages are processed by OpenRouter and its partner model providers. Please review the OpenRouter Privacy Policy to understand how they handle prompt data.

6. Children's Privacy

Arithmaxa does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided data through AI Chat, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Security

All local data is protected by your device's operating system and browser security. Transmissions to the OpenRouter API use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

8. Local Storage vs Cookies

Arithmaxa uses browser localStorage, not traditional HTTP cookies. localStorage data is stored entirely on your device and is not automatically sent to any server with HTTP requests, unlike cookies. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings or using the "Delete All My Data" button above.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and notify you within the app if the changes are material. Continued use of Arithmaxa after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
